## Algarve Community leaders gathering

People building spaces are normally by themselves and ironically lack a tribe feeling. Someone to ask questions to. This is why I love [flourishmode](https://www.instagram.com/flourishmode.co/) and [Fábrica de Olhão](https://www.instagram.com/afabricadeolhao/)'s initiative to actually just create a space for an informal meet to happen. We're looking to keep the momentum going, discussing what we need help with and learn from each other. If you'd like to be involved, reach out!

## Social impact

People care, no matter what others say. Between [Algarve Up! Social and Academia Novos Líderes](https://www.instagram.com/anjealgarve/) (both ANJE projects in the Algarve focused on civic impact and training a new generation of leaders), [Farol Incubator](https://open.spotify.com/show/3WAZqYuutNA9NmyL3K92vl) and their Monday podcast exploring impact, [Faro em Ação](https://faroemacao.pt) pushing for youth to get involved with their city, and [Anraga Health](https://www.instagram.com/anragahealth/) that combines dance and science to improve older adults' lives, everyone you can find associations, communities, incubators, and businesses actively creating impact and inspiring the next wave.

## A founder's journey

It seems cliché to say, but the tiniest of actions and intros can change people's journey in a big way.

We're all connected. What does [Limaro](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lana-ivanova-6201079_founderinstitute-limaro-startuplife-share-7467622870588923905-sMlt/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_ios&rcm=ACoAAA5mNQkByky2vl8gigesIDLL_7ZW-S3DnZs), [Closure.](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2026-06-08/closure-takes-global-second-place-at-techstars-global-pitch-competition/1036715) and [Tail Trails](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tail-trails-club_the-tailtrails-team-had-the-chance-to-participate-activity-7468015432680153089-Y0Ec?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_ios&rcm=ACoAAA5mNQkByky2vl8gigesIDLL_7ZW-S3DnZs) have in common? All of them were part of a Startup Weekend, and all of them have this curiosity of what comes next, how far can you go?


## Running, books, new bauhaus

I read (sometimes). I don't run (yet) but I can walk. And I love seeing people promote art. The world is waking up and making social and active culture mainstream again.[Books for Brunch](https://www.instagram.com/booksforbrunch.club/) brings the girlies to Fnac, [casual runs](https://www.instagram.com/p/DZVia6DDMxw/?igsh=YXU4bXVldHVidGY4) with the promise of a burger I could one day get behind, [summer sunset walks in Quarteira](https://luma.com/5r72kp5g) with some lofties, [Albauhaus in Faro](https://www.instagram.com/albauhaus_algarve/) showing a different side of the city.

## Community map goes online

Biased, but I'm proud to say you can see a little of what we mapped in March for [THE COMMUNITY MAP](https://local.foundation/communitymap) online. The work continues over on the [ecosystem map](https://local.foundation/ecosystem) where we've been adding new info every week. We're focused more than ever to research community development and understand how to measure the great impact it has in our regions.

## Aldeias do Futuro

I wasn't prepared to be devastated and deeply inspired by an hour-long documentary by [Re-Rural](https://www.re-rural.info/). It was a hard watch, because we've spent too long focused on our artificial cities, forgetting what community really means, helping your neighbour, believing in a region. And yet, we have so much potential to do better again.
